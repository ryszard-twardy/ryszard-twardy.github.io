Data analyst, Erlangen

SQL and Power BI, with Python for the parts a spreadsheet cannot do

I am a data analyst based in Erlangen, Germany. I work in SQL and Power BI, with Python for the parts a spreadsheet cannot do. I am at my best where the data is messier than the question: master data with the same location spelled a dozen ways, exports nobody has profiled, a report whose numbers cannot be traced back. I do the whole chain myself, from the SQL that cleans the data to the semantic model to the page a manager reads, and I keep the reasoning written down so it survives me.

Career

  • Data analyst, independent projects, Erlangen, April 2026 to present. A master data cleansing pilot for a building-materials group: 26,256 SAP ship-to records resolved to 1,991 delivery locations, seven left for manual review. Kupferkanne, a customer-analytics build on synthetic data in BigQuery and Power BI. synth-datagen, an open-source Python data generator.
  • Retraining, November 2025 to March 2026. Full-time on BigQuery, Power BI and DAX; the two builds above are the output.
  • Drone Survey Project Manager, MapCad3D GmbH, Erlangen, October 2023 to October 2025. UAV LiDAR and photogrammetry surveys for photovoltaic projects, and an Excel and Power Query reporting workflow that consolidated CSV outputs into comparison tables and statistical summaries and cut the reporting cycle by about half.
  • UAV Strategy Implementation Manager, AIR6 SYSTEMS / AIRBORNE ROBOTICS, Klagenfurt, June to July 2023. CE marking and C-class implementation strategy for manufactured UAVs under EU Regulation 2019/945.
  • GIS specialist, junior to lead, Ojców National Park, Poland, November 2006 to May 2023. Designed and ran the park's GIS and data infrastructure; regional data coordinator for the Natura 2000 site PLH120004.

Tools

Warehouse and SQL: BigQuery, GoogleSQL, dimensional modelling. BI and modelling: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, PBIP/TMDL, Tabular Editor 2, Excel. Python and tooling: pandas, openpyxl, pytest, MkDocs, Quarto. Version control: Git, GitHub, GitHub Actions.

Languages: Polish (native), English (fluent), German (A1, in progress).

How I work

I write the reasoning down: Kupferkanne has an architecture decision record for every major choice, so a reviewer can see why a measure exists, not just that it does. I re-derive numbers before I publish them, usually two ways. I use AI-assisted tooling for code and drafts; the design, the tests and the responsibility for what ships are mine, and the repositories are public so that can be checked.

Before analytics

Seventeen years in GIS came first. I joined Ojców National Park as a junior GIS specialist in 2006 and left as lead GIS specialist in 2023, having designed its GIS infrastructure, managed geodatabases that combined raster, vector and tabular data, and secured funding for airborne LiDAR and orthophoto coverage of around 400 square kilometres. Two years of drone surveying in Germany followed, flying LiDAR and photogrammetry missions for photovoltaic projects. What carried over: heterogeneous sources, formal reporting, and documentation written for the next person.

Education

Postgraduate Diploma in GIS, UNIGIS (Jagiellonian University, Kraków, and University of Salzburg), 2009 to 2011. M.Sc. in Geography, GIS focus, Jagiellonian University, 2001 to 2006.

Outside work

I photograph landscapes, mostly panoramas and often from the air, and build and backtest systematic trading strategies for the pleasure of being proved wrong by data.
